Gym Tracker privacy policy

Gym Tracker is a personal app for logging workouts, meals and habits.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads steps, distance, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, weight and workouts from Health Connect, and writes the body weight you enter. This data is used only to show your daily progress and give training tips inside the app.

Health data is stored on your phone. It leaves the phone only if you turn on sync with your own Supabase project, and then only to that project. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Your control

You can remove the app's access at any time in Health Connect settings, and delete your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
